SOURDOUGH

Why Noble Sourdough?

Fermented
Naturally fermented, improving flavor and digestibility.
Lower GI
Releases sugar more slowly, helping maintain stable blood sugar levels.
Less Inflammatory ATI’s
Fermentation helps break down certain wheat proteins that may cause irritation.
Easier to Digest
Long fermentation breaks down gluten and starches, making it gentler on the stomach.
More Nutrients
Fermentation improves the body’s ability to absorb important minerals.
Less Processed
Made using traditional baking methods without industrial shortcuts.
Minimal Ingredients
Typically made with just flour, water, salt, and natural starter.
